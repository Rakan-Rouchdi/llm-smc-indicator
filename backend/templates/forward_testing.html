Observation protocol

WIN: entry touched after the decision, then TP1 before SL. LOSS: entry touched, then SL before TP1. EXPIRED: no entry touch before the decision expiry. UNKNOWN: missing data, unresolved sequence, or both levels touched without reliable ordering.

Record the chart source, timezone, observation window and event timestamps in evidence. Review through decision expiry for entry eligibility and up to 24 hours after the decision for an entered paper setup; unresolved results stay UNKNOWN. Corrections append to the audit history.

API acceptance is not proof of phone receipt. Paper observations do not prove fills, costs or profitability. No retrospective entries before the decision, and no trades are placed.
